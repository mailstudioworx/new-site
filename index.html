About African Photos & Videos

We celebrate African visual storytelling by showcasing the work of talented photographers and videographers from across the continent. Our mission is to connect viewers with authentic, high-quality content and direct them to professional licensing platforms where they can license or purchase images and videos from the creators.

Every gallery item links to creator portfolios on Getty Images, Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, and Pond5—supporting fair compensation and professional recognition.
